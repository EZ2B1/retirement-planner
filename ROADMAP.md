# Roadmap

## Milestone 1 — Calculation foundation
Federal tax, Social Security, portfolio, pension, RMD, and projection engines.

## Milestone 2 — Healthcare interactions
Medicare, IRMAA, ACA premium-tax-credit, and MAGI logic.

## Milestone 3 — Strategy optimization
Roth conversions, Social Security claiming, withdrawal sequencing, and guardrails.

## Milestone 4 — Workbook production
Professional Excel workbook, audit checks, dashboards, CSV exports, and documentation.

## Milestone 5 — Validation and release
Regression testing, independent review, tax-year update process, and versioned releases.
